framework on top of the existing baseline Frame3DSolver. Per project scope:

    for LC1-LC9 or any of the 30 combinations below.

    this load-case/combination/viewer layer.

    load arithmetic is internally correct (sum of loads == intended total;
    combination total == independently re-derived total). They are NOT a
    structural equilibrium check against support reactions.
